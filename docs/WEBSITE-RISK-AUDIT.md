# BeSportify website privacy, consumer protection and accessibility audit

Reviewed: 13 September 2026. Scope: this repository’s corporate website. This is an implementation and risk assessment, not legal clearance or a guarantee against claims. No deployment or external CMS edits were performed.

## Release position

The policy pages and technical safeguards are implemented. **Do not treat the site as legally cleared for launch or payment collection.** The operator’s identity, jurisdiction, public address, monitored email contacts, actual data retention and commercial terms have not been supplied. The location `Asia/Kolkata` is only a research clue, not proof that Indian law governs the business. Serving people elsewhere can bring additional laws into scope.

The form remains disabled unless a legal name, public business address, country and valid email contact are configured. Incomplete policies are marked `noindex`; this is not an access restriction or a compliance substitute. Once those details are present the form prepares an email draft, not a server submission. No checkout has been added.

### Business facts still required

Supply these through approved Sanity site settings or the documented variables in `.env.example`:

- Legal operator name, public business/service address, country/state and registration number where applicable. A brand name alone does not establish the operator’s legal identity. Do not publish a personal home address without deciding whether an alternative lawful service address is available.
- Monitored support and privacy email addresses; named grievance contact where required. Verify the mailbox works and assign responsibility for responding.
- Countries served, B2B/B2C scope, whether children use the services, and the actual connection to the separate StatStrike application.
- Payment channels, prices/taxes, renewals, fulfilment, cancellations, refund eligibility, refund method, timelines and grievance escalation. The current refund page accurately describes a corporate site with no checkout and preserves mandatory rights; it is not a completed policy for a paid subscription.
- Hosting/CDN and email providers, regions, contractual safeguards, backups, operational log settings, and actual retention/deletion schedules. The privacy page does not invent a “30-day deletion” promise. Confirm and make the published retention criteria more specific before collecting live enquiries.

## Storage inventory and consent decision

| Storage                                         | Trigger                                    | Purpose and retention                                | Control                                                              |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `besportify-theme` in first-party local storage | User checks “Remember my theme”            | Stores only `light` or `dark`; no automatic expiry   | Turn off the checkbox, clear saved theme, or clear browser site data |
| `sanity-preview-perspective` cookie             | Authorised editor enters validated preview | Requested preview perspective; one hour              | Expires or editor exits preview                                      |
| Editor workspace authentication                 | Access to Sanity Studio                    | Provider-specific authentication/workspace operation | Review Sanity settings/notices independently                         |

**Current code decision:** no public analytics/advertising banner is needed for features that are absent. Saving appearance is optional and explicit. This conclusion depends on the deployed host matching the audited code. Before adding non-exempt tracking or third-party embeds, implement blocking before consent, equally accessible acceptance/rejection, purpose choices and withdrawal; never reinterpret the appearance preference as tracking consent. UK guidance now includes an appearance exception with clear information and a simple free objection mechanism. Other jurisdictions need their own assessment. [ICO exceptions](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-the-use-of-storage-and-access-technologies/what-are-the-exceptions/), [ICO consent implementation](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-the-use-of-storage-and-access-technologies/how-do-we-manage-consent-in-practice/).

## Jurisdiction assessment for legal review

These are conditional issues, not a declaration that every listed law applies.

| Jurisdiction/topic               | What needs checking                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| India: DPDP transition           | The November 2025 commencement notification staggers the Act: many core processing, consent, rights and children provisions commence 18 months after Gazette publication, rather than all taking effect immediately. The final Rules also use staged commencement. Confirm the exact publication-based dates and any later amendments before launch; prepare purpose-specific notices, request handling, processor arrangements, safeguards and age/guardian handling as applicable. [Act commencement notification](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/c56ceae6c383460ca69577428d36828b.pdf), [final Rules](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf).                                                 |
| India: current IT privacy duties | Do not assume the DPDP transition removes existing IT Act/SPDI obligations. Review applicability to the actual entity and data, including health/injury information that sports products might process. This corporate form expressly discourages those details. [Government clarification of the 2011 Rules](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/erelcontent.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=74990).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| India: sales and refunds         | Assess Consumer Protection Act and E-Commerce Rules applicability across the separate app, invoices and online offerings. Required disclosures and redressal processes cannot be replaced by a generic “no refunds” clause. The government announced 2026 amendments on **10 September 2026**, effective **1 January 2027**, including NCH convergence, complaint copies, sponsored listing/price transparency and dark-pattern audit requirements. Assess scope and implementation dates with counsel rather than applying future duties as current ones. [2020 Rules](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/sites/default/files/E%20commerce%20rules_0.pdf), [2026 government announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2308759&lang=1&reg=48). |
| India: marketing                 | Substantiate outcome statements and endorsements; disclosures cannot repair a materially misleading claim. Review customer permission and material connections before releasing proof. [CCPA misleading advertisement/endorsement guidance announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1832906&lang=2&reg=48).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| India: cybersecurity             | Assess CERT-In directions for the operator/provider, including specified incident reporting and log retention duties. Do not promise deletion that conflicts with mandatory security records. [CERT-In directions and official FAQs](https://www.cert-in.org.in/Directions70B.jsp).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Accessibility                    | Use WCAG 2.2 AA technical checks and assess the local legal duties of the actual service. In India, review the RPwD Act, applicable rules/standards and current judicial directions; this code audit is not that legal determination. [W3C WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/), [RPwD Act](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2155?locale=en).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| EU/EEA and UK                    | An overseas operator may fall under GDPR when offering goods/services to people there or monitoring behaviour; mere global availability is not the complete test. Assess representative requirements, lawful bases, transfers, rights handling and local cookie rules. Paid consumer products also need a cancellation/digital-service review. [GDPR, especially Articles 3, 13, 27 and Chapter V](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=celex%3A32016R0679).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| California and other US states   | Determine the business’s audience, revenue, processing scale and sale/sharing practices before selecting privacy obligations. CCPA has scope thresholds; its inflation-adjusted revenue threshold is $26,625,000 for 2025–2026. Other state privacy, consumer, accessibility and children’s rules can apply independently. [California AG overview](https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa), [California Privacy Protection Agency thresholds](https://privacy.ca.gov/laws-and-regulations/monetary-thresholds-in-the-ccpa/).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Copyright and trademarks         | Having an image file, crediting a creator or generating an image with AI does not establish all necessary rights. Obtain applicable copyright licences and releases; check sports/team marks, dataset/database licences and screenshots containing player data. [Indian Copyright Office handbook](https://copyright.gov.in/documents/handbook.html).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |

## Validation and operational follow-up

- Production build, TypeScript and ESLint checks completed; automated tests cover opt-in/withdrawal, no-JavaScript form safety, minimal enquiry data, email encoding, keyboard controls, image gating and GROQ proof filtering. See `docs/audit-results` for browser results when available.
- Before deployment, verify live headers, HTTPS, cookies/local storage and every network destination on a clean browser visit, after rejection/withdrawal and when using any embed. Hosting or proxy injection is outside a source-only search.
- Test representative pages with VoiceOver/NVDA, keyboard only, zoom/reflow and both appearance modes. Recheck every approved CMS image’s actual alt text, tables and headings.
- Configure and exercise privacy/grievance responses, deletion including backups, provider contracts and security incident procedures; policies alone do not implement those operations.
- Review and audit the separate StatStrike app before using this website as evidence that the product is compliant. Sports/player data and children’s information increase the scope materially.
- Repository housekeeping: `git status` failed before changes with `fatal: .git/index: index file smaller than expected`. The index was not repaired or reset, and no commit was created. Back up and repair the Git index separately before relying on version-control status.
