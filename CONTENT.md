## 3. Content status labels

Use these labels inside documentation and CMS preparation. Never display them publicly.

- `[APPROVED-DRAFT]`: strategically approved wording; may still need normal proofreading
- `[VERIFY]`: claim or feature must be checked against a reliable internal source
- `[SUPPLY]`: BeSportify must provide factual information or an asset
- `[PERMISSION]`: publication requires consent or usage approval
- `[LEGAL]`: requires privacy/legal review
- `[OPTION]`: alternative wording, not final copy
- `[HIDE-UNTIL-READY]`: omit the entire section until requirements are met

2. Cricket is its current focus.

4. BeSportify also provides analytical and technology services.

Do not start with a long list of dashboard features before establishing the company and business outcome.

## 7. Voice and tone

### 7.1 Voice attributes

- Clear
- Informed
- Evidence-led
- Practical
- Confident
- Curious
- Respectful of sporting judgement

### 7.2 What the voice is not

- Overly academic
- Full of unexplained analytics jargon
- Aggressively sales-led
- Predicting certainty where none exists
- Dismissing coaches, scouts, or human judgement
- Inflated with unsupported superlatives

### 7.3 Writing patterns

Prefer:

> Understand how a player performs across roles, phases, and match situations.

Avoid:

> Unlock revolutionary, AI-powered, next-generation player optimization.

Prefer:

> StatStrike supports decision-makers with structured evidence.

Avoid:

> StatStrike guarantees winning decisions.

### 7.4 Grammar and style

- Use Indian/British English consistently: `organisation`, `analyse`, `optimise`
- Use sentence case for headings and buttons
- Prefer active voice
- Keep sentences generally under 25 words
- Use contractions sparingly in corporate copy
- Use numerals for metrics, but only when verified
- Explain abbreviations at first use
- Avoid exclamation marks in primary marketing copy

## 8. Approved and prohibited terminology

### Approved terms

- Sports technology
- Sports intelligence
- Cricket intelligence
- Performance analysis
- Opposition preparation
- Player evaluation
- Scouting and selection support
- Match intelligence
- Decision support
- Data-driven products
- Analytical solutions
- Evidence-backed decisions

### Use with qualification

- `AI-powered`: only when a specific active AI capability is described
- `Predictive`: include inputs, purpose, and limitations
- `Real-time`: only when the feature actually updates at the required latency
- `Official partner`: only with documented league/organization approval
- `Trusted by`: only when approved clients and relationships support the wording
- `Impact`: distinguish evidence from correlation or anecdote

### Avoid without proof

- Best
- Leading
- Number one
- Most advanced
- Guaranteed
- Unbeatable
- Revolutionary
- Industry-first
- Match-winning
- Official league partner

## 17. Case-study detail template

### Required structure

1. Title
2. Client/team/organisation
3. Exact relationship description
4. Competition/date/context
5. Challenge
6. Why the question mattered
7. Approach
8. Data and scope
9. Intelligence/deliverables
10. How the client applied it
11. Verified outcome
12. Limitations/confidentiality
13. Approved testimonial
14. Relevant product/service
15. CTA

### Writing rules

- Start with the client's decision or problem, not BeSportify's tools
- Distinguish deliverables from outcomes
- Quantify only with evidence and a defined period
- Explain what BeSportify contributed without claiming responsibility for an entire sporting result
- Remove tactical, medical, selection, and player-sensitive details unless explicitly cleared
- If the client cannot be named, describe the context accurately without creating a fictional identity

### Example framing—not a factual case study

Challenge:

> The team needed a faster, more consistent way to prepare player and opponent information before upcoming fixtures.

Approach:

> BeSportify structured the available match data around defined tactical questions and presented the findings through focused analytical views.

Outcome:

> `[SUPPLY verified evidence of how the output was used or what changed]`
